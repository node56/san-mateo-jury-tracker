How this data was collected

  • Source: the reporting status table on the court's Juror Services page.
  • Schedule: a live-browser check Sunday–Thursday at 6:00 PM PT, the evening before each service date, plus a few 11:15 AM call-back checks. Since Nov 25, 2026 both checks are automated by a GitHub Action (.github/workflows/fetch-jury.yml, script in scripts/fetch_jury.py): 6:00 PM PT Sun–Thu for the evening posting, 11:20 AM PT Mon–Fri for the call-back outcome. If the table cannot be fetched or parsed, the run fails and writes nothing — no group status is ever guessed.
  • Why a live browser: the table lives in an iframe on web.sanmateocourt.org — a plain-text fetch misses it entirely. On Mon Nov 23 at 11:15 AM that iframe host timed out (11:14–11:43 AM), so that call-back outcome is recorded as not verified, not guessed.
  • Scope: groups 1000–1019 only, the cohort listed during this period. Other weeks list different group numbers.
  • Machine-readable data: data.json — same facts as this page, one entry per service date.

What the pattern looks like so far

Seven of the eight evening postings were identical: no group called, all 20 on call-back. The exception was Monday Nov 23 — the first day in the period with groups actually called: 1000–1003 at 8:30 AM and 1004–1007 at 9:30 AM, at the Hall of Justice and Records, 400 County Center, Redwood City. At the 11:15 AM call-backs that were checked, both Mon Nov 16 and Tue Nov 17 ended with all 20 groups excused ("Your appearance is not required"). That is a pattern in two weeks of one court's standby pool, not a prediction — the next cohort's groups are different people, and a single trial can change a day.
